# Gym Floor Planner

**[▶ Try it in your browser](https://freddysalem.github.io/gym-floor-planner/)**

A to-scale, drag-and-drop floor planner I built during my internship at **Obermeyer Helika** (Prague, June 2026) to lay out the fitness centre on the second level of the Novo Plaza extension.

Gym layouts were being estimated by eye around columns, walls and windows. This tool draws the real floor plate to scale, so every layout is accurate and quick to change.

- **56 equipment types** at real manufacturer dimensions, dragged from a library onto the floor.
- Free rotation, multi-select, resize, snap to a metric grid, pan and zoom.
- Off-floor warnings, properties panel for exact sizes and positions.
- **Save and load layouts as JSON**; export PNG or SVG to share.

One self-contained `index.html`. No install, no build step, and it runs offline.
